## Working agreements
1. Pull request reviews: Every pull request is reviewed by at least one other member within 24 hours on weekdays. Nobody merges their own PR.
2. Response time: Every member replies to a direct message or @mention in the team chat within 12 hours (8:00-22:00), even if the reply is only “I’ll look at this tomorrow.”
3. Meetings: The team meets twice a week for 30 minutes (for example Tuesday and Friday at a fixed time).
4. Work items: Every task is a GitHub Issue with one assignee before anyone starts coding. Each member updates the status of their issues at least once every 3 days.
5. Conflict handling: If two members disagree for more than 15 minutes, they stop and ask the Scrum Master to make a call, and the result is written in decisions.md.

## Decision rules
- Product questions: Decided by Product Owner after consulting the team
- Technical questions: Decided by majority vote among Developers
- Deadlock (no agreement after 24 hours): Decided by the Scrum Master 
- Where decisions are recorded: lab01/decisions.md
