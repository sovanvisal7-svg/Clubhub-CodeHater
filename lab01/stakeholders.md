# Stakeholders · ITC Club Hub (v0.1, 2026-__-__)
| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Needs from Club Hub | How we reach them |
|-------------|----------|-----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| ★ Students | High | Medium | An easy way to find active clubs, check upcoming event details, and see announcements without scrolling through spam. | Chat with classmates on campus, run quick Google Form surveys, or post a poll on existing student discord channels. |
| ★ Club leaders | High | High | A straightforward way to post events, manage member sign-ups, and cut down on tedious paperwork. | Sit down with a few club presidents over coffee or drop by their weekly coordination meetings. |
| ★ Our team | High | High | Clear coding requirements, everyone pulling their weight, and a layout that doesn't break when we connect features. | Daily Discord syncs, sharing a Trello task board, and doing code walkthroughs before merging features. |
| Club organizers / committees | High | Medium | A clean calendar layout so they don't accidentally schedule major events on top of each other. | Drop a message in their main organizer group chat or invite them to look at our initial interface drafts. |
| Campus life administrators | Medium | High | A way to monitor new club approvals and make sure room bookings comply with university rules. | Schedule a brief meeting during office hours or send a structured email update to our faculty advisor. |
| The client / Professor | High | High | A project that actually works, well-structured C++ code, and clear documentation that shows we followed instructions. | Regular check-ins during weekly lab sessions and formal milestone demonstrations. |
| Institute IT staff | Low | High | Reassurance that our app won't hog local server resources or compromise student data privacy. | Submit a quick system overview document or ask them for their standard security guidelines. |
