## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to compare three software development process models:

1. Waterfall
2. Incremental
3. Scrum-style iterative

The team uses individual scoring, agreed criteria weights, and weighted calculations to select the most suitable process model for ITC Club Hub.

Five team members independently scored the three process models from 1 to 5.

### Differences Between Individual Scores

There were some differences in individual opinions:

- Kheng Daniza preferred Scrum-style because it supports changing requirements, frequent feedback, and sprint-based teamwork.
- Soeung Mengheng and Lay pichrattanak preferred Incremental because it provides structured development and allows the team to deliver features step by step.
- Siem Khattyavatey and Ouk Sovanvisal also preferred Incremental because it supports regular testing and gradual delivery.

### Team Discussion

After comparing the five individual score sheets, the team found that Incremental received the highest combined score.

Although Scrum-style received a higher score from one member, most members preferred Incremental because it provides a clear development structure without requiring frequent Scrum ceremonies.

## 3. Criteria and Weights

The team agreed on the following criteria and weights.

| Criterion | Weight | Justification |
|---|---:|---|
| Requirements stability | 20% | Core club and waitlist features are already clear, although some details may change. |
| Client availability | 15% | Client feedback is available periodically at project milestones. |
| Team experience | 20% | The team is more comfortable developing features step by step. |
| Deadline | 25% | The team must complete the project and submit it by Week 14. |
| Risk | 20% | Early testing and integration can help identify problems before final submission. |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

## 4. Agreed Team Scores

After discussing individual opinions, the team agreed on the following scores.

Scale: 1 = poor fit, 5 = excellent fit.

| Criterion | Weight | Waterfall | Incremental | Scrum-style |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Requirements stability | 20 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Client availability | 15 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Team experience | 20 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Deadline | 25 | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| Risk | 20 | 2 | 4 | 4 |

### Score Justification

- **Requirements stability:** Scrum-style is highly adaptable, while Incremental also allows changes between development stages.
- **Client availability:** Incremental supports feedback at planned checkpoints without requiring constant client participation.
- **Team experience:** Incremental provides a manageable structure for the team's current development experience.
- **Deadline:** Incremental allows important features to be completed in stages before the final deadline.
- **Risk:** Incremental testing and early integration help detect problems before they affect the entire system.

## 5. Weighted Score Calculation

## 7. Conclusion

Based on the five individual scoring sheets and the team's weighted comparison, Incremental is the preferred process model for ITC Club Hub. It allows the team to develop and test working features in structured stages while receiving feedback at planned milestones. One weakness is that integrating different increments can become difficult if the system architecture is not clearly planned. The team can reduce this risk through early architecture planning, regular integration testing, and clear communication. The team will also use Scrum-style sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives to improve coordination. However, if there is a problem that may emerge, change will be taken into consideration.
