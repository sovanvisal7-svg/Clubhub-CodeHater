## Individual conclusion

I think **Incremental** is the best process model because **it allows our team to deliver working features in structured stages, adapts well to periodic feedback, and reduces integration risks without requiring daily Scrum overhead**.

One weakness of this model is **that poorly defined system architecture early on can cause refactoring issues when integrating later increments**.
