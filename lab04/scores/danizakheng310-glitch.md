## Individual Conclusion

I think Scrum-style is the best process model because it encourages teamwork, regular communication, and continuous feedback. It also allows the team to adapt to changing requirements and prioritize important features during each sprint.

One weakness of this model is that it requires regular communication and active participation from all team members. Without good coordination, the team may struggle to complete sprint tasks on time.
