**Decision:** MIT License
**Rationale:**

The team proposes the MIT License because it is a simple and permissive open-source licence that allows students, developers and companies to use, modify and distribute ITC Club Hub. It does not require future teams to publish their improvements, making it suitable for educational projects and future development. Although Apache-2.0 provides an explicit patent grant and GPL-3.0 requires certain distributed modifications to remain under the same licence, MIT offers greater simplicity and flexibility. It is also compatible with GoogleTest, which uses the BSD-3-Clause licence. The final decision will be confirmed after checking the course and institute requirements with the teaching assistant.
